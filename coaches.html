How we work

Your programme, our support

Your head coach keeps control of the training plan. We make sure the halls, mats, partners and recovery slots are ready when your session starts.

  • Session plan agreed with your coaching staff before arrival
  • Local sparring partners matched by weight and level
  • Guest coaches for specific disciplines on request
  • Strength, conditioning and recovery blocks built around mat time
