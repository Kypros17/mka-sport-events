Why Cyprus

The Mediterranean advantage

1

Reliable weather

More than 300 sunny days a year means outdoor sessions are never cancelled — even in January.

2

Easy to reach

Direct flights from most European capitals to Larnaca and Paphos, with short transfers to all our venues.

3

Quality facilities

Modern halls, certified mats, grass and hybrid pitches, athletics tracks and fully equipped gyms.

4

Value for money

Competitive package prices, especially outside the summer peak — ideal for winter and spring camps.
