Who we are

Built by people who live and breathe sport

We started on the mat, not behind a desk. Our team has competed, coached, refereed finals and run championship venues — so when a club asks about warm-up halls, mat thickness or transfer times, the answer comes from experience rather than a brochure.

Everything we do happens in Cyprus. That is deliberate: we know the halls, the hotels, the federations and the people who make an event run, and that local knowledge is what turns a booking into a camp that actually works.

  • Based in Nicosia, working across the whole island
  • Trusted partner of clubs and federations in Cyprus
  • One contact for planning, logistics and delivery
Leadership & affiliations

Founded and led by Marios Andreou

MKA sport Events was founded by Marios Andreou and is run by him today. In the sport itself he serves as President of the Cyprus Sambo Federation, the national governing body for sambo on the island and a member of the European Sambo Federation (ESF) and the International Sambo Federation (FIAS).

That presidency is his own role in sambo rather than a position held by the company. It does shape how we work, though: competition rules, the fixture calendar, referee and officiating practice and the technical standards a championship hall has to meet are all familiar ground for our team.

It is also why we follow closely as Cyprus keeps attracting world-level sambo to the island:

  • European Sambo Championship — Cyprus, 2025
  • World Cadet, Youth and Junior Sambo Championships — Larnaca, 2024
  • First World University Sambo Championships — Nicosia, 2016

These are FIAS and ESF championships hosted in Cyprus, listed here as background on the island's record as a host. They were not organised by MKA sport Events, and no endorsement of the company by the Cyprus Sambo Federation, the ESF or FIAS is implied.
