How it works

From enquiry to collection in four steps

1

Tell us your plans

Discipline, dates, venue and the mat area you need — a quick message is enough to start.

2

Get your quote

We confirm availability and send a fixed quote covering mats, transport and installation.

3

Flexible packages

Rent for a day, a weekend or a season

Every event is different, so pricing is always a tailored quote — but these are our typical rental formats:

  • Day rental — gradings, seminars and one-day tournaments
  • Weekend rental — championships and multi-day opens
  • Long-term rental — camps, academies and seasonal venues
  • Event package — mats plus scoreboards, barriers and podiums

Delivery and installation are available across all of Cyprus, including weekends and evenings.
